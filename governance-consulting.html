Governance & consulting

My interest in governance and strategy grew when I took up my title as Music Director of Symphony Nova Scotia, attending all Board meetings, eagerly contributing to annual reports, strategic plans and artistic strategy, and learning an immense amount from my colleagues in the process.

‍

Over the same period, i co-founded, served as a Director and then Co-Chair of the Board for formidAbility, the UK's first majority disability-led opera company. In that time, the company established itself as one of the most respected disability-positive arts organisations in the UK. Founder and CEO Joanne Roughton-Arnold's pioneering practice, which places accessibility at the heart of the creative process, is setting a new industry standard.

‍

I was Artist-in-Association at English Touring Opera from 2020-2022, contributing to discussions on equitable hiring practices and advising on diversity and artistic matters, and in 2025, I joined the Artistic Council of the OAcademy, extending a relationship with that organisation that began as a guest teacher. In that capacity, I advise on the artistic and academic goals of the academy, its place within the wider music training ecosystem, and the ways in which emerging tech might impact the educations and careers of the students.

‍

After my Music Directorship ended at Symphony Nova Scotia, I accepted a role as Artistic Advisor, while the orchestra engaged in a search for a new conductor. I am delighted to continue my association with the orchestra, working alongside Director of Artistic Administration, Eric Mathis and the orchestra's new CEO, Scott Tresham to ensure continuity in the interim, and maintain a connection between programming and the orchestra's strategic plan and community-focused mission.

‍

I've now been appointed to a similar role, Artistic Consultant, at the National Youth Orchestras of Scotland, an organisation with which I've been associated for 10 years now.

‍

With a full portfolio of work and family commitments, I am not in a position to take on a further board position at this time, but I welcome approaches from organisations interested in a shorter term consultancy relating to artistic programming, technology or strategic planning.
